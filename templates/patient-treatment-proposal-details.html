Medical Assessment & Treatment

Case Review Summary

Initial review based on provided information: Patient presents with multi-vessel coronary artery disease based on the provided angiography report and clinical history. The medical board has reviewed the ECG and recent blood panels. A surgical intervention is recommended to bypass the blockages and restore optimal blood flow.

Proposed Treatment Plan

Proposed Treatment / Procedure
Minimally Invasive Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)
Treatment Approach
Minimally invasive approach utilizing a smaller keyhole incision rather than traditional sternotomy. This approach aims to reduce recovery time, lower the risk of post-operative complications, and minimize scarring. The procedure will involve grafting 2 to 3 vessels depending on the final intraoperative assessment.
Treatment Stages
1
Initial Evaluation: Physical consult, pre-anesthesia checkup, and updated ECG/Echo upon arrival.
2
Procedure: Minimally invasive CABG surgery (Estimated duration: 4 hours).
3
Recovery: 2 days in Cardiac ICU observation followed by private room recovery and initial physiotherapy.
